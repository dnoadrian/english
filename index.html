Verb 01 / 03 could generelle Fähigkeit / Unmöglichkeit

could

Allgemeine Fähigkeit in Vergangenheit, Zukunft & Present Perfect
Past — positiv
She could swim when she was five.
Past — negativ
They couldn't take the exam at their school.
Future
One day, everyone will be able to access the internet.
Present Perfect
We have been able to solve more problems since then.
Alle Formen
Past ✓could + Infinitiv
Past ✗couldn't + Infinitiv
Future ✓will be able to + Infinitiv
Future ✗won't be able to + Infinitiv
Pres. Perfect ✓have/has been able to + Infinitiv
Pres. Perfect ✗haven't/hasn't been able to + Infinitiv
could nur für allgemeine vergangene Fähigkeit. Bei einem konkreten einmaligen Erfolg → was able to!
Verb 02 / 03 was/were able to konkrete einmalige Situation

was/were
able to

Konkrete Fähigkeit in einer bestimmten Situation
Past — konkret ✓
We were able to stop them this time.
Past — konkret ✗
They weren't able to get their jobs back.
Future
My brother will be able to print 1,500 leaflets.
Present Perfect
We haven't been able to discuss everything yet.
Alle Formen
Past ✓was/were able to + Infinitiv
Past ✗wasn't/weren't able to + Infinitiv
Future ✓will be able to + Infinitiv
Future ✗won't be able to + Infinitiv
Pres. Perfect ✓have/has been able to + Infinitiv
Pres. Perfect ✗haven't/hasn't been able to + Infinitiv
⚠️ Bei einmaligem Erfolg in der Vergangenheit NUR was/were able to, nicht could!
She was able to win the race. ✓  ·  She could win the race. ✗
Verb 03 / 03 was/were allowed to Erlaubnis — von außen gegeben

was/were
allowed to

Durfte / durfte nicht — Erlaubnis in allen Zeiten
Past ✓
From 1897, women were allowed to attend university.
Past ✗
But they weren't allowed to study all subjects.
Future ✗
They won't be allowed to have a supermarket there.
Present Perfect ✗
Women haven't always been allowed to do what men have.
Alle Formen
Past ✓was/were allowed to + Infinitiv
Past ✗wasn't/weren't allowed to + Infinitiv
Future ✓will be allowed to + Infinitiv
Future ✗won't be allowed to + Infinitiv
Pres. Perfect ✓have/has been allowed to + Infinitiv
Pres. Perfect ✗haven't/hasn't been allowed to + Infinitiv
allowed to = Erlaubnis (jemand anderes gibt/gab sie)  ·  able to = Fähigkeit (kommt von innen)
